Status Light

Status Light is a personal tool that runs on one Mac. It turns a desk lamp busy, focus, or free and shows today's calendar on a small panel and a smart display in the same home.

It reads calendar events through the Google Calendar API with read-only access, so it can show the day and know when a call starts. It never creates, changes, or deletes events.

It is not a public product and is not offered to other users.
