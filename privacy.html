Privacy policy

Last updated September 26, 2026.

Status Light is a personal tool used by its developer only.

What it reads

With your permission, it reads your Google Calendar events (title, time, calendar, attendees, and video links) using the read-only scope calendar.readonly. It uses them only to draw the day view and to set the lamp during calls.

Where the data goes

Events and the sign-in token stay on the Mac that runs the tool, in the user's own config folder. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's, nothing is sold or shared, and nothing is used for ads or to train models.

Removing access

Revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the tool's config folder removes everything it stored.
